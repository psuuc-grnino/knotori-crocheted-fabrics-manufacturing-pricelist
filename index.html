Hello! I'm Geraldine, the founder, designer, and sole hands behind KNOTORI. Every piece in this catalog begins as a simple loop of yarn and transforms stitch by stitch into a piece of art made with endless care and attention.

My crochet philosophy centers on slow, intentional craft using soft milk cotton, chenille, and durable acrylic blends. Whether I'm stitching cozy wearable wardrobe items, cute little keychains, or detailed plush amigurumis, my goal is to bring cozy warmth to your day.

Geraldine's Yarn Care Tips

Gentle Hand Wash

Wash in cool water using mild detergent. Avoid harsh scrubbing or twisting the stitches.

Air Dry Flat

Lay flat on a clean towel in a shaded area. Never hang wearable crochet as it may stretch the fibers!

Plushie Fluffing

Gently reshape your amigurumi or plushies after shipping or washing to keep them looking squishy.
